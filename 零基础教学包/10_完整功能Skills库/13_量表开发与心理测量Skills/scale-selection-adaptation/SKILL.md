# Scale Selection And Adaptation

Use this skill before collecting survey or experiment data that uses latent constructs.

## Inputs

- Construct definitions.
- Research context and target population.
- Candidate scales or papers.
- Language and cultural context.
- Target journal standards.

## Workflow

1. Define each construct conceptually and operationally.
2. Search or verify validated scales using real sources.
3. Compare candidate scales by construct fit, item count, context fit, reliability history, and citation quality.
4. Plan translation, back-translation, expert review, and pilot testing when needed.
5. Specify response anchors, item order, reverse coding, and attention checks.
6. Produce a measurement table.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not invent scale sources or psychometric evidence.
- Do not mix items from different constructs without theoretical justification.
- Do not treat translation as validation.
- Flag items that overlap too closely with manipulations or dependent variables.
