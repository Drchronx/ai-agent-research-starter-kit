# Causal Claim Ladder

Use this when deciding how strong the manuscript language can be.

## Claim Strength

1. Association: observational relation without identification.
2. Predictive association: model predicts Y but does not identify treatment effects.
3. Quasi-causal evidence: identification strategy plus assumptions and diagnostics.
4. Experimental causal evidence: randomized treatment with valid manipulation.
5. Mechanism evidence: design supports X -> M -> Y, not merely a cross-sectional indirect path.

## Language

- Association: "is associated with", "correlates with", "predicts".
- Quasi-causal: "provides evidence consistent with a causal effect, conditional on assumptions".
- Experiment: "increased/decreased" or "caused" only when randomization and manipulation validity are adequate.
- Mechanism: "consistent with the proposed mechanism" unless mediator is experimentally manipulated or temporally identified.
