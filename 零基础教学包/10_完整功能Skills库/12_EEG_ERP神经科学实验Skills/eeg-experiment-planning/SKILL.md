# EEG Experiment Planning

Use this skill before data collection or when auditing an EEG/ERP study design.

## Inputs

- Research question and theory.
- Paradigm: oddball, Go/No-Go, Stroop, flanker, decision task, consumer choice, HCI interaction, BCI task, or custom paradigm.
- Target components or metrics: P1, N1, N2, P3, LPP, N400, FRN, ERN, alpha, beta, theta, connectivity, classification accuracy.
- Hardware, montage, sampling rate, event trigger plan, participant population, and exclusion rules.

## Workflow

1. Translate the theory into measurable neural and behavioral outcomes.
2. Define event markers and time-locking events.
3. Specify montage, reference, sampling rate, impedance target, and recording environment.
4. Plan task timing, trial counts, counterbalancing, blocks, breaks, and artifacts risk.
5. Define preprocessing and analysis plan before data collection.
6. Produce a preregistration-ready design summary.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not claim a component measures a construct unless the task timing and literature support it.
- Do not overpromise source localization, connectivity, or classification from weak data.
- Keep behavioral, EEG, and event-marker outputs synchronized.
