# SEM CFA Path Latent Models

Use this skill when constructs are latent, models contain multiple simultaneous paths, or measurement error must be modeled.

## Inputs

- Theoretical model.
- Item-level or scale-level data.
- Measurement model and structural paths.
- Software preference.

## Workflow

1. Confirm whether SEM is necessary or whether regression is sufficient.
2. Specify measurement model before structural paths.
3. Check sample size, missing data, distribution, and estimator choice.
4. Report CFI, TLI, RMSEA, SRMR, chi-square, degrees of freedom, and model comparison when appropriate.
5. Test structural paths, indirect effects, and alternative models.
6. Write measurement and structural results separately.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not tune model fit with unexplained correlated errors.
- Do not treat good global fit as proof of theory.
- Do not compare latent means without measurement invariance.
- Do not hide poor loading items or model modifications.

## Output

```text
SEM suitability:
Measurement model:
Structural model:
Estimator and missing-data handling:
Fit table:
Path table:
Alternative models:
Reporting paragraph:
```
