# PROCESS Mediation Moderation

Use this skill when the research question involves mechanisms and boundary conditions.

## Inputs

- X, Y, mediator, moderator, covariates, and model diagram.
- Dataset and variable coding.
- Whether variables are experimental, measured, longitudinal, or cross-sectional.
- Planned PROCESS-style model or theoretical model.

## Workflow

1. Verify that the design can support the proposed causal language.
2. Map theory to model paths.
3. Choose mediation, moderation, moderated mediation, or serial mediation.
4. Specify covariates before looking at significance.
5. Estimate direct, indirect, conditional, and interaction effects with uncertainty intervals.
6. Report simple slopes, Johnson-Neyman regions, and conditional indirect effects when relevant.
7. Separate confirmatory and exploratory mechanism tests.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not claim causal mediation from cross-sectional measured variables without caveats.
- Do not require a significant total effect before testing indirect effects.
- Do not interpret interactions without simple effects or marginal effects.
- Do not hide alternative model directions.
