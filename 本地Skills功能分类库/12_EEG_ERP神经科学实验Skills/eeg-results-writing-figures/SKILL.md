# EEG Results Writing And Figures

Use this skill to convert analysis outputs into manuscript-ready Methods, Results, figures, and limitations.

## Inputs

- Preprocessing log.
- Trial-retention and QC metrics.
- ERP/frequency/connectivity/ML outputs.
- Target journal or reporting style.

## Workflow

1. Write preprocessing in enough detail for replication.
2. Report artifact handling, retained trials, channels, ICA decisions, and exclusions.
3. Report planned components, ROIs, time windows, and models.
4. Create a figure plan: waveforms, topographies, ERSP, connectivity, classification metrics.
5. Interpret neural effects in relation to task timing and theory.
6. Add limitations and robustness checks.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not overinterpret component labels as direct psychological constructs.
- Do not hide preprocessing attrition.
- Do not omit non-significant planned tests.
- Do not present exploratory windows as confirmatory.

## Output

```text
Methods paragraph:
Results paragraph:
Figure plan:
Table plan:
QC disclosure:
Interpretation limits:
Reviewer-risk checklist:
```
