# Scenario Experiment Design Checklists

## Stimulus Checklist

- Same length and tone across conditions.
- Only the intended treatment changes.
- The manipulation appears before mediator and DV measures.
- No condition contains extra information about competence, risk, cost, quality, blame, privacy, or moral severity unless these are intended manipulations.
- Scenario is realistic for the target sample.
- The participant role is clear.
- The decision or judgment is concrete.

## Measure Checklist

- DV maps directly to the research question.
- Mediator is conceptually distinct from the manipulation and DV.
- Moderator is measured or manipulated before it can be affected by the treatment.
- Scale anchors are explicit.
- Items are not double-barreled.
- Reverse-coded items are used cautiously.

## Validity Checklist

- Internal validity: randomization, confound control, clean manipulation.
- Construct validity: validated scales, clear operationalization.
- External validity: realistic context, relevant participants.
- Statistical conclusion validity: sample size, reliability, effect sizes, assumptions.
